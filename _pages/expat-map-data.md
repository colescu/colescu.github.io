The visited-area boundaries on the [Expat Life map](/expat/) use these sources:

- **Salzburg:** [Austria's municipality boundary service](https://gis.geosphere.at/maps/rest/services/grenzen/admin_grenzen_oesterreich/MapServer/2), © BEV 2017. The municipality polygon was converted to GeoJSON.
- **Bratislava:** [Slovak administrative boundaries](https://www.gku.sk/geoportal-en/zbgis/download/), © GKÚ Bratislava, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The 17 city borough boundaries were reprojected and merged into one outline.
- **Corinth:** [2021 Greek municipal units](https://sdigmap.tee.gov.gr/mapping/rest/services/UDM/UDM_SERVICE_ELSTAT/MapServer/5), ELSTAT / TEE. The Corinth municipal unit was converted to GeoJSON.
- **Messinia and Argolida:** [Greek regional-unit boundaries](https://geohub.necca.gov.gr/server/rest/services/ELBIOS-EXTERNAL_DATA/perifereiakes_enotites/MapServer/0), NECCA. Both regional units were converted to GeoJSON.
- **Other visited regions:** [Natural Earth administrative boundaries](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/).
- **New York City and Princeton:** [2025 US Census place boundaries](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.2025.html).

The base-map providers are credited in the map's attribution control.
